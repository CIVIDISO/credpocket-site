CREDPOCKET

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Last updated October 13, 2026

CredPocket is made by Grimoire LLC. In short: your credentials stay on your phone, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't keep your photos.

What we process

  • Document photos you choose to scan. The photo is resized on your phone and sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI service to read the details (course title, hours, dates, license number). We don't store the photo or the extracted details on our server. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to be used to train its models. You can skip AI scanning entirely and type details yourself.
  • A random app ID. Created on first launch so we can count your free scans. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID or device.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, the app sends Apple's signed transaction so our server can confirm it. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

Your licenses, documents, photos, CE records, reminders and credential packets are stored only on your iPhone (and in your iCloud backup if you use one). We can't see them. When you share a credential packet, you choose where it goes using the iPhone share sheet.

Health information

CredPocket is for your own professional credentials. It isn't designed for patient or client information, so please don't scan documents containing it. CredPocket isn't offered on behalf of a healthcare provider or employer.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics tools, and no tracking across apps or websites. We treat everyone the same whether or not "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control is set.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • Links to your board of nursing open their website, which has its own privacy policy.

Retention

Our server keeps only the random app ID and a count of free scans used. Standard server logs (time, request type, network address) are kept briefly for security and are automatically rotated out.

Your choices

Use Settings → Delete all my data to erase everything in CredPocket on your phone, or delete the app. To have your free-scan record removed from our server, open an issue asking for it (don't post your app ID publicly) and we'll explain how to send it privately; we'll respond within 45 days. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Children

CredPocket is for licensed professionals and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if the policy changes. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
