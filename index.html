CREDPOCKET

Your licenses, CE and certifications. Always renewal-ready.

CredPocket is an iPhone app for nurses. Snap a photo of a CE certificate, BLS/ACLS card or license, and it reads the hours and dates for you, checks your state's renewal rules, reminds you before anything expires, and builds a credential packet you can send to a registry, agency or new employer.

How it works

  • Add your license: state, type (RN, LPN/LVN, APRN, CNA) and expiration date.
  • Scan documents: CredPocket's AI reads the details and you confirm them before anything is saved.
  • See what's left: hours completed this cycle and the required topics your state asks for.
  • Send your packet: one PDF with your licenses, CE summary and copies of each document.

Common questions

Which states' CE rules are built in? Florida, Texas, California, New York, Ohio, Georgia, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, Washington and Arizona, for RNs and LPN/LVNs. More are coming. In other states CredPocket still tracks your hours and every expiration date.

Are the rules official? They're summarized from each state board's published rules (checked October 2026) and linked to the source. Rules change, so always confirm with your board of nursing before renewing. CredPocket isn't affiliated with any board or with CE Broker, and it doesn't report CE for you.

Where are my documents stored? On your iPhone. When you scan, the photo is sent to our server only to be read, then discarded. Deleting the app deletes your documents, so keep the originals too.

What does Pro include? Unlimited AI scans (fair use: up to 60 a day) and the credential packet. Free includes 5 AI scans, unlimited manual entries, rule checks and reminders.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CredPocket, at least 24 hours before renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
